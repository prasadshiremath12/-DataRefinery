🚀 Day 1/21: Welcome to The Data Refinery!

Ever wondered why "Data Engineer" wasn't a job title in 2005?

📜 A Brief History:

1990s: Everything lived in SQL databases (Oracle, MySQL). BI developers wrote complex stored procedures.

2006: Yahoo open-sourced Hadoop (HDFS & MapReduce) to handle massive scale. The "Big Data" era was officially born.

2014: Apache Spark arrived, moving execution into memory and making processing up to 100x faster than Hadoop.

Today: Cloud Warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery) + Lakehouses (Delta Lake, Iceberg) mean software engineering meets data processing.
